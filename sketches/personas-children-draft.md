# Children and young people: draft personas

Made-up personas for challenge 2: children and young people in the "circle of support" around someone who uses drugs or alcohol. They're built from the barriers on the challenge sheet, not from real people or research.

**Keep in mind:** anything for children needs safeguarding built in, a clear route to a trusted adult, and language they understand. A real service would need specialist advice on this.

## 1. Ellie, 9: lives with her dad

- **Situation:** her dad drinks most evenings. She often puts herself to bed.
- **The moment:** 9pm on a school night. Her dad has fallen asleep on the sofa and won't wake up properly. She's scared.
- **What gets in the way:** she doesn't know who to tell, and she's worried her dad will get in trouble or she'll be taken away.
- **She needs:** to know what to do right now, in words she understands, and to know it's not her fault.

## 2. Kai, 13: the one who looks after things

- **Situation:** his mum uses drugs. He looks after his little brother and keeps it quiet at school.
- **The moment:** late on a Friday. His mum hasn't come home and isn't answering.
- **What gets in the way:** he doesn't trust adults with it, he has no phone credit, and he'd never call a helpline in case someone finds out.
- **He needs:** a private way to ask for help that doesn't feel like reporting his mum, and some support for himself.

## 3. Sophie, 16: worried about her older brother

- **Situation:** her brother, 20, has started using more since he lost his job. Their parents don't know.
- **The moment:** 1am. He's messaged her something that worries her.
- **What gets in the way:** she promised not to tell, she doesn't know which services are open at night, and she thinks she should be able to handle it.
- **She needs:** guidance on how to help him without breaking his trust, and to know when it's time to get an adult or emergency help.
