# Family and friends: draft personas

Made-up personas for challenge 2. Each one is built from a barrier on the challenge sheet, not from real people or research.

## 1. Morag, 58: a mum in a rural village

- **Situation:** her son, 31, drinks heavily and goes quiet for days. The nearest service is an hour's drive away.
- **The moment:** 11pm on a Saturday. He's not answering his phone and she's frightened.
- **What gets in the way:** everything is shut until Monday, and she's not sure it's "bad enough" for 999.
- **She needs:** someone to talk to now, and to know what she can do tonight.

## 2. Jay, 24: a friend and flatmate

- **Situation:** his flatmate uses drugs at weekends. Jay is the one who's there.
- **The moment:** 2am. His flatmate is unsettled, and Jay doesn't know whether it's normal or getting worse.
- **What gets in the way:** worry about getting his friend in trouble, low trust in official services, and 8% battery.
- **He needs:** quick, plain guidance that works on a dying phone, with no judgement.

## 3. Priya, 42: a sister, from a distance

- **Situation:** her brother lives in another town, and she's his main support.
- **The moment:** a worrying text late on a Sunday evening.
- **What gets in the way:** she doesn't know which services exist where he lives, and support varies by area.
- **She needs:** to find out who could reach him tonight, and support for herself, because she's exhausted.
