# Challenge 2: who we're helping

Notes from Yasmin Tasker (Business Analyst). A draft for the squad to discuss. Rebecca, as product owner, makes the call and records it in `TEAM.md`.

## Yasmin's note

> We are helping services that support people who use drugs and alcohol to provide support to these people at times where this help is currently missing: out of hours.

**User group (proposed):** support workers, charities and community organisations.

**Framing to steer clear of a service directory:** focus on *coordination*, not listing services. For example: *How might out-of-hours workers hand someone on to another service tonight, so they're never left without anyone?*

## Comparing the three user groups

The first row is from the challenge sheet. The other two are judgement calls, so challenge them.

|---|---|---|---|
| **What the sheet says** | At their most vulnerable and isolated at night; stigma and low trust | Often don't know how to help or where to turn | Support is spread across many organisations; services need to pool cover |

## Draft problem statements

1. **People who use drugs or alcohol, at night:** A person alone at night can't reach support they trust, because most services are closed and they're wary of statutory ones. They need someone non-judgemental to turn to, so they feel safer and things don't escalate into a crisis.
2. **Family and friends:** A family member worried about someone late at night doesn't know how to help or where to turn, because support closes at 5 and is spread across many organisations. They need guidance and support for themselves, so they can help without calling 999.
3. **Support workers:** Out-of-hours support workers can't see what help other organisations offer tonight, because support is fragmented and varies by area. They need to pool cover with other services, so fewer people fall through the gaps.
