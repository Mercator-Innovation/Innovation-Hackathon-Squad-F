# Turtle Toast: business analysis pack

Backing documents for the squad's prototype. They aren't for show and tell. They record what we built, why, and what a real service would still need.

**Squad:** JJARY (Squad F)
**Challenge:** Challenge 2: Help when it's needed
**Prototype:** `src/` (Turtle Toast, a hub that plugs into a pretend game app called Blockville)
**Written by:** Yasmin Tasker (Business Analyst), 22 October 2026, from the prototype as it stood on `main` that afternoon

## What's in the pack

| Document | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [Business case and scope](business-case.md) | The problem, who it's for, goals, what's in and out of scope, stakeholders, how we'd measure success |
| [Requirements](requirements.md) | User stories with acceptance criteria, business rules, non-functional requirements |
| [Process flows](process-flows.md) | The user journeys and the safe adult chat, as diagrams |
| [Data and privacy](data-and-privacy.md) | What the prototype stores, where, and what a real service would need |
| [RAID log](raid-log.md) | Risks, assumptions, issues, dependencies and open questions |
| [Traceability](traceability.md) | Each requirement mapped to the persona it serves, the screen and file, and its status |

## Ground rules this pack follows

- **Prototype only.** Nothing here describes a live service. People, volunteers and conversations are made up.
- **Helpline details are real.** The squad checked them on each service's own website on 22 October 2026. They can change.
- **No real personal data.** The personas in `sketches/` are invented, built from the barriers on the challenge sheet.
- **Our users:** families and friends of people who are showing signs of harmful drug or alcohol use. This prototype focuses on the children and young people among them. On screen, for children, we say "someone you love".
