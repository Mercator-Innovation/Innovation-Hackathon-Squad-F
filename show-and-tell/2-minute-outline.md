# JJARY: 2-minute show and tell

## 0 to 60 seconds: what we built, and for whom

- **Turtle Toast**: a safe space hub for **children and young people** whose family member is showing signs of harmful drug or alcohol use. It hides inside a game they already play (Blockville): tap the Turtle Toast pop-up and you're in. **Quick exit** drops you straight back to the game.
- Show it: **What do I do?** opens a little world to walk around, where each house has help for a moment like "They won't wake up properly". There's also **Text a safe adult**, **Who's open now?** (open or closed, tonight), **What happens if I tell?** and an offline page that works with no signal. (Jordan also built a Roblox version.)

## 60 to 90 seconds: one thing that didn't work

- **Chat to someone** didn't survive. Next to "Text a safe adult" it confused things, so we took it out and pointed its links to the safe adult.
- We had no real children to test with, so our personas (Ellie 9, Kai 13, Sophie 16) are made up. We also found no evidence on what families need at night specifically.

## 90 to 120 seconds: one surprise from the tooling

- **Everyone built outside their job.** The business analyst built the "Help right now" screen and the animations. The service designer designed the logo and ran the squad vote. The user researcher turned the fake game into a playable one. The QA engineer built the walk-around world's garden, stars and dog, and ran accessibility checks.
- **More than 60 changes merged in under 4 hours, with almost no clashes.** Claude did all the git work, and QA still caught real bugs, like Skip to content sending you back to the game.
